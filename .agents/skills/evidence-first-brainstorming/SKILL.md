# Evidence-First Brainstorming

This skill is for discovering problem spaces and opportunity hypotheses. It is **not** for implementation, architecture, framework selection, or protecting an incumbent product concept.

## Workflow

1. **Problem evidence** — Collect concrete signals that a problem exists, who experiences it, and its scale/severity. Prefer primary or independent sources.
2. **Prior art** — Find existing products, research systems, student projects, pilots, and failed attempts before proposing an answer.
3. **Root cause** — Separate visible symptom, constraint, and underlying mechanism; state uncertainty.
4. **Divergence** — Generate many fundamentally different directions across domains. At least 40% must be outside generic AI tutoring/LLM education products when that is the incumbent anchor. Do not generate minor chatbot feature variants.
5. **AI necessity test** — Ask: “Would this work almost as well without AI?” Penalize candidates where the answer is yes.
6. **Novelty attack** — Search deliberately for products, papers, and student projects doing the same thing. A missing search is not evidence of novelty.
7. **Failure attack** — Test adoption, data, privacy, safety, regulation, evaluation, operational burden, and demo-only failure modes.
8. **Convergence** — Rank only after the preceding evidence is collected. Score problem significance, novelty gap, AI necessity, evidence strength, evaluation feasibility, MVP feasibility, demo strength, and differentiation.

## Evidence discipline

- Mark every statement as FACT, EVIDENCE, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, or IDEA.
- Prefer Tier 1 sources (peer-reviewed work, original datasets, government/intergovernmental reports, official competition archives, technical documentation), then Tier 2 (universities/nonprofits/proceedings), then Tier 3 (companies/journalism). Use forums only as a signal.
- Seek contradictory and negative evidence. Do not turn absence of evidence into confirmation.
- An opportunity space is a problem/user/mechanism/evaluation combination—not a feature list or a chatbot persona.
- Do not build anything or choose technology while using this skill.
