# Product-loop observations

The strongest surviving loops share a visible transformation:

`real capture (photo/video/audio/scan) → learned semantic interpretation → human-verifiable action → outcome/label feedback`

Examples: crop image to farm-action review, equipment image to inspection evidence, child speech to a re-read cue, sports video to a revised movement, environmental audio to a ranger/analyst queue, or archive scan to a cataloger-approved record.

Weak loops repeatedly collapsed into one of four shapes:

1. `form → database → dashboard` — better as conventional workflow software;
2. `constraints → schedule/route` — better as CP-SAT or graph optimization;
3. `sensor threshold → alert` — better as validated rules/signal processing;
4. `upload/question → generated text` — model output is presentation unless it creates a reliable downstream decision.

High-stakes records can have compelling inputs but still fail a credible student-product loop because clinical, legal, emergency or regulated responsibility cannot move to a model. In such records, a human accountable reviewer is a hard boundary, not a superficial safety disclaimer.
