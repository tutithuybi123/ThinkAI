# Emerging opportunity clusters (not a shortlist)

Clusters preserve neighboring workflows for later comparison; they are not proposed products or ranked directions.

| Workflow family | Representative records | Typical inputs | Current-tool limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accessing and completing public/service interactions | P001–P003, P085, P093–P100 | forms, speech, scans, schedules | people bridge formats and systems manually |
| Field observation into a trustworthy action | P004–P016, P026, P031–P040, P109–P116 | photos, maps, sensor/time series, reports | sparse data and manual interpretation/routing |
| Inspection, condition and quality evidence | P046–P053, P103–P108 | images, video, observations | subjective judgement, fatigue, and poor finding-to-action handoff |
| Cross-organization information handoff | P041–P045, P054–P062, P073–P080 | documents, messages, records | duplicate entry, disconnected records and unclear ownership |
| Resource, material and waste decisions | P063–P072, P122–P125 | meters, inventory, receipts, item images | records are dispersed; recurring waste is hard to see |
| Accessibility and assisted participation | P017–P022, P086–P102, P121 | text, audio/video, layouts, individual routines | inaccessible formats, missing support capacity, uncertain conditions |
| Learning, coaching and feedback | P084, P089–P092, P103–P108 | work samples, speech, video, histories | individualized review is time-limited |
| Community/heritage/tourism context | P035–P036, P082–P083 | venue/route information, requests, local context | information and service capacity gaps |

The most visible later test question is whether each family’s hard step is semantic/perceptual or instead better handled by workflow design, data integration, rules, or optimization. That test has intentionally not yet been applied.
