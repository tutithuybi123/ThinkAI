# Rolling-wave evidence intake (post-Wave 0)

They were deduplicated against the 125-row canonical CSV at a **workflow** level; obvious duplicates (for example, generic flood alerting, generic visual maintenance, and generic shelter-record software) were not counted as additions. These are not candidate products or rankings.

## Source and inference boundary

Every table row has source evidence for the underlying workflow/pain. The model relevance supplied by workers is a **researcher inference**, not evidence of efficacy. High-consequence health, laboratory, aviation, emergency and justice records explicitly require human accountable review and are preserved primarily as problem evidence, not build directions.
