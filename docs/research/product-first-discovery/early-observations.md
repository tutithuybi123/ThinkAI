# Early observations — no shortlist

- Repeated credible friction is often not “lack of information,” but transforming mixed evidence into a specific, accountable next action: an inspection finding into a work order, a report into a routed case, or a warning into an accessible action.
- The naturally model-heavy input classes found so far are field photos/video, speech, inaccessible scanned material, environmental observations, and high-volume media. This is only a preliminary signal; models still need evaluation and safe handoff design.
- Several rich areas are visibly classical-first: scheduling interpreter/service appointments, routing known categories, itinerary/route optimization, inventory rotation, data interoperability, and energy management given reliable instrumentation. A learned model might be useful only at an unstructured-input boundary.
- Repeated weak pattern to avoid: attaching a generic conversational layer to existing public information, study material, safety advice, or a checklist without changing the underlying workflow outcome.
- Vietnam-specific sources are especially strong for public access, rural/agriculture records, disability inclusion, informal work/waste, fisheries, transport and disaster preparedness. Local validation is still necessary before asserting a local product need beyond the source claim.
- Wave 1 is deliberately probing thin domains rather than adding more variants of education, generic tutoring, or generic document chat.
- Safety-and-realtime Wave 1 adds an important boundary: many high-stakes alerts have explicit regulatory thresholds or irreducible false-alert/latency tradeoffs. Those are useful real problems, but they should not be treated as autonomous-AI opportunities.
