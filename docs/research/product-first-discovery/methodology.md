# Methodology

## Purpose and unit of analysis

Each record names a person or organization doing an observable task, the existing workflow/workaround, its pain, naturally occurring inputs, and a useful action or outcome. Themes such as “education is inefficient” were excluded. This is a high-recall discovery atlas, not a demand estimate or a product plan.

## Evidence discipline

`SOURCE-EVIDENCED` means a cited source directly supports the problem, constraint, affected user, or current workflow. `MIXED` means the source supports the surrounding problem, while the detailed workflow is a researcher inference explicitly indicated in `notes`. Source strength is High for government, intergovernmental, official standards, peer-reviewed reports, or systematic reviews; Medium for credible sector/NGO reports or applied case studies; Low–Medium for practitioner workflow sources.

Sources were used for discovery rather than to infer market size, local adoption, model performance, legality, or novelty. Vietnam relevance is `direct` only where the cited evidence is Vietnamese; `transferable` means the workflow may be relevant but needs local validation.

## Collection and deduplication

Four non-overlapping source slices were searched: Vietnam/public and rural workflows; global operations; human-facing workflows; and the prior repository evidence. Near-identical users and outcomes were merged; adjacent stages of a workflow were kept where a person experiences a distinct decision or failure. Every row is one of 125 numbered IDs and contains a source reference in the ledger.

## Deliberate exclusions

No proposed AI solution is developed beyond a one-sentence relevance signal. We did not eliminate problems because an existing product might exist, a data source is unknown, or a model is not obviously necessary. Medical records are restricted to administration, coordination, or human-reviewed navigation—not autonomous diagnosis or treatment.

## Next stage (only after review)

Human review may choose a broad subset for serious non-AI-versus-AI comparisons, model-necessity, product-loop, demo, data, and feasibility tests. Scores and product directions must not be inferred from this atlas.
