# Elimination report

## Eliminated

- **Generic THINKAI / AI learning assistant:** commercially and academically saturated; AI explanations/hints are not sufficient learning outcomes. Keep no platform/MVP claim.
- **Auditable school/community information provenance:** no verified local recurring problem; a transparent issuer/timestamp/archive ledger is a stronger non-AI baseline than an AI fact-checker.
- **Privacy-minimal school-route exposure:** AI necessity is below the stated survival threshold; personal-exposure ground truth and child-location privacy are disproportionate.

## Not eliminated, but severe kill conditions

- **Environmental QA:** currently borderline; eliminate if no reference/collocation or labeled fault ground truth exists.
- **Provenance:** eliminate if stakeholders cannot identify recurring consequential source-provenance failures or a rules-based ledger suffices.
- **Accessibility:** eliminate generic checker; eliminate the narrow variant without expert-labelled semantic corpus or co-design.
- **Misconception experiment:** currently borderline; eliminate if fixed taxonomy/rubric cannot be teacher-reviewed or delayed transfer cannot be measured with appropriate school/parent consent.
- **Route exposure:** eliminate if local calibration data is unavailable, if child location retention is needed, or if health claims would be implied.

## Competition evidence boundary

Four individual official ISEF ProjectBoard abstracts were deeply usable: [ElSa](https://projectboard.world/isef/project/85971), [BiDEx](https://projectboard.world/isef/project/82840), [motor design](https://projectboard.world/isef/project/etsd014---investigating-a-novel-electric-motor-design?postID=322781), and [empathy replication](https://projectboard.world/isef/project/beha015-differential-empathy-and-social-dominance-sdo). Conrad/Technovation individual public pages were insufficient in this pass; absence is not novelty evidence.
