# Candidate 06 — Current THINKAI / generic AI learning assistant baseline

**Status: ELIMINATE as currently framed.**

Commodity components: Socratic chat, hints, answer withholding, explanations, quizzes, personalization, mastery dashboards, knowledge tracing. They exist across Khanmigo, general LLMs, Duolingo Max, Quizlet, MATHia, ALEKS, ASSISTments, and many others. The research literature supports particular mechanisms but not a generic chat platform; results depend on comparator, implementation, and delayed transfer.

Only a narrow scientific remnant deserves further investigation: a fixed misconception taxonomy plus teacher-authored hint policy, benchmarked against deterministic rules/static hints/answer bot, with delayed-transfer and calibration outcomes. This is Candidate 04, not the current THINKAI product direction. No system architecture, MVP, or platform scope follows from this conclusion.
