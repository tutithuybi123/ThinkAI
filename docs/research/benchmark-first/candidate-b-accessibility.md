# B — Semantic accessibility auditing

**Status: ELIMINATED.**

[DocLayNet](https://github.com/DS4SD/DocLayNet), [PubLayNet](https://github.com/ibm-aur-nlp/PubLayNet), and [PubTables-1M](https://github.com/microsoft/table-transformer) provide layout/table labels, not end-to-end semantic accessibility, meaningful alt-text, PDF tag semantics, or assistive-technology usability ground truth. Existing deterministic validators such as [PAC](https://pac.pdf-accessibility.org/) are strong rule baselines.

Phase-3 reviewer evidence also identifies a 2025 PDF Accessibility Benchmark with expert-validated criteria and rules/LLM/VLM/hybrid evaluation; repository terms/files require verification before reuse. Therefore a generic hybrid auditing claim is saturated. A distinct expert-labelled task, licensed corpus, fixed split, and clear improvement over validators would be required to reopen this candidate.
