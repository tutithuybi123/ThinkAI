# Prior art and commodity boundary

## Frontier baselines

| System/category | Already does | What remains for ThinkAI |
|---|---|---|
| [ChatGPT Study Mode](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11391654-chatgpt-business-release-notes) | Interactive questions, Socratic guidance, progressive explanations, personalization (official feature claim) | Cross-session assistance ledger, isolated transfer tests, delayed evidence and experimental intervention comparison are not established by the cited feature description. |
| [Gemini Guided Learning](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/guided-learning) | Guided learning, questions, step-by-step help and multimodal learning resources (official feature claim) | Google also reports a preregistered 2026 Sierra Leone mathematics RCT with 1,763 students; ThinkAI cannot claim differentiation from “AI that improves learning,” only from assistance-aware independent verification ([LearnLM official research page](https://cloud.google.com/solutions/learnlm)). |
| General frontier chat | Excellent explanation, task generation, file/image understanding and memory | A learner can request the answer, evaluator isolation is absent, and model memory is not an auditable learner model. |

## Education platforms

| System/category | Already does | Commodity verdict |
|---|---|---|
| [Khanmigo](https://www.khanacademy.org/khan-labs) | Socratic guidance, answer withholding, Khan content integration, critical-thinking prompts | “AI that does not give the answer” is commodity. |
| [ALEKS](https://www.aleks.com/about_aleks/research_behind) | Knowledge-space learner state, adaptive assessment, readiness, individualized paths | Concept/prerequisite maps, mastery states and adaptive next-topic selection are mature prior art. ALEKS also describes periodic reassessment for retention ([official description](https://www.aleks.com/about_aleks/HowALEKSWorks_TextDescription)). |
| MATHia/ASSISTments/IXL/CENTURY/Riiid/knowledge tracing | Skill models, hints, practice selection, dashboards and prediction | Learner modeling and adaptive practice alone are not differentiation. |
| Duolingo/Anki/Quizlet-style practice | Spacing, retrieval, difficulty adaptation, streaks | Spaced review and gamified progression are commodity. |
| Custom research AI tutors | Expert-authored scaffolds, correct solutions and constrained tutoring can produce learning gains | ThinkAI must compare against a purpose-built guarded tutor, not a weak chatbot. |

## Closest competitor

ALEKS is closest to the proposed observe–model–path–reassess concept. Khanmigo is closest to the guided conversational experience. Together they cover most of the original concept. ThinkAI has no reason to exist if it merely combines them.

## Potential structural gap

The reviewed products foreground tutoring, mastery or progress. ThinkAI can foreground a different contract:

> The system distinguishes evidence produced under assistance from evidence produced independently, then deliberately changes assistance and task context to test what remains.

That combination is not declared novel from absence of search results. Its differentiation must be demonstrated behaviorally: a frontier tutor or adaptive platform should be able to show the same assisted solution, while ThinkAI exposes a different, more accurate conclusion about independent capability and selects a better next action. Google's reported Guided Learning RCT makes the baseline substantially stronger; an ordinary “pedagogically prompted AI tutor” is no longer enough to justify ThinkAI.

## Commodity list to avoid presenting as innovation

* chat/Socratic prompts;
* hints instead of answers;
* learner profiles and concept maps;
* prerequisite graphs;
* knowledge tracing/mastery percentages;
* adaptive quizzes and learning paths;
* spaced repetition;
* misconception labels;
* teacher dashboards;
* generated practice questions;
* multiple agents/models.

The innovation claim, if earned, is the **measurement contract and closed loop**, not these components.
