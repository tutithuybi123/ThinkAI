## Honest 3-minute demonstration

1. A student completes a problem with escalating AI support; the ordinary success screen would say “correct.”
2. ThinkAI labels it **assisted success—not yet independently verified**.
3. It presents a new, verified near-transfer challenge without AI. The learner's response changes the evidence state.
4. Show a pre-recorded, timestamped delayed return from a prior session and the learner-state update.
5. Display benchmark evidence from held-out tasks and the guarded-tutor comparison—not a cherry-picked anecdote.

The memorable contrast is behavioral, not visual polish.
