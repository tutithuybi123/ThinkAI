## Patterns, not a formula

The 20 records are sharply bounded, name a beneficiary, and pair a technical mechanism with an observable outcome. AI is strongest when it analyzes sensing, prediction, or experimental data that rules/manual review cannot reasonably handle. Health, autism and mental-health titles are **not** evidence that those are low-risk team opportunities; they demonstrate crowded prior art and demanding validation.
