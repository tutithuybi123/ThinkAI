* [Candidate C — Crisis Claim-to-Source Triage](candidate-c.md): converts a forwarded crisis claim into a time- and location-bounded evidence card grounded in authoritative updates, or explicitly reports that confirmation was not found.

Candidate B has the strongest evaluation story. Candidate C has the strongest immediate public-safety story, but also the highest operational and harm risk. Neither is a final selection. [Head-to-head](head-to-head.md) retains Candidate A as the current leader.
