# Full Product vision

ThinkAI is a challenge-first learning evidence product for learners who use AI and need an honest answer to: **what have I actually shown that I can do independently in a changed situation?** It is not a generic chat tutor, a mastery percentage dashboard, or an automated diagnosis engine.

## Mature learner loop

Across skills and chapters, the learner chooses a goal, attempts a task, receives legitimate assistance, solves, then meets an isolated reviewed new-situation challenge. A qualifying result yields a narrow Capability Receipt; a later retrieval check can add time-qualified evidence. Progress explains what has been observed, what remains untested, and the next useful action. Later failure adds conflicting evidence and recovery work; it never erases an earlier event or labels the learner permanently.

External learning may be recorded only as self-reported context or an imported source with explicit provenance. It is never silently treated as independent evidence.

## Direction boundaries

| Category | Decision |
|---|---|
| Committed direction | condition-aware assistance record, reviewed changed-situation verification, append-only evidence, truthful receipt/history, learner growth language, teacher review/audit |
| Conditional research | multi-skill/chapter sequencing, delayed retrieval scheduling, prerequisite probes after repeated evidence, abstaining misconception hypotheses, intervention comparison, lightweight teacher summaries, later multi-subject support |
| Explicitly rejected unless evidence/ADR changes | Learning Twin, global risk prediction, personality/learning-style labels, automated ground-truth misconception claims, generic chatbot-first UX, mastery percentages, AI Assistance Debt, XP/coins/streaks/leaderboards, open-world game, unreviewed AI ground truth, broad adaptive path, multi-agent platform |

## Teacher role

Teachers review task pairs, answer/rubric and intervention versions; approve/withdraw content; inspect audit records; and correct interpretations through append-only correction events. A teacher mode may be a compact evidence-review surface, not a full LMS by default.
