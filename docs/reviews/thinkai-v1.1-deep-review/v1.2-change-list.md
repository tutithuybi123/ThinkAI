## Explicit kill/reframe criteria

Reframe or kill if any of these is met: a strong tutor prompt plus generated quiz/reminder matches outcomes and perceived value; reviewers cannot validate task-pair equivalence at usable cost; exposure tags lack acceptable rater agreement; ledger does not beat final correctness at predicting held-out independent outcomes; learners avoid hints or call the trial extra homework; no real teacher/user decision changes from the ledger; a close competitor demonstrates the complete controlled loop; consented delayed evaluation is infeasible; or the demo cannot explain the difference without a long theory lecture.

## Reinstating older ideas only after evidence

Keep detailed learner profiles, global risk prediction, Learning Twin language, and broad adaptive paths removed. Add a **small prerequisite index** only after repeated independent failure identifies a discriminating follow-up task; treat misconception diagnosis as a ranked hypothesis with correction/abstention, never a label; consider intervention comparison only after enough repeated evidence exists to compare later independent outcomes. These features could be a future differentiator because they would be conditioned on better evidence, but none belongs in the first validation prototype.
