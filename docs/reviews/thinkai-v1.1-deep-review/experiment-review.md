# Experiment review

## Separate studies

### A. Measurement validity

Question: does the condition-aware ledger predict an independently administered delayed outcome better than final correctness alone?

Use a prospective, teacher-reviewed item bank. Hold out both delayed items and at least some item families. Pre-register outcome, features, and the simple baseline. Compare discrimination and calibration; report confidence intervals. A protocol that uses the same transfer item to update and evaluate itself is contaminated. The smallest serious pilot estimates feasibility, rater agreement, attrition, and variance; it cannot establish a stable predictive advantage without a power analysis based on pilot effect/variance.

### B. Learning effect

Question: does the ThinkAI policy improve independent later performance versus a strong tutor policy?

Randomize learners (parallel design preferred for one topic; counterbalanced crossover only with different, validated equivalent skills and washout/order control). Same model, model version, content coverage, problem count, allowed time, interface attention, reminder schedule, and delayed-test timing. The control must have high-quality Socratic tutoring and generated practice; otherwise the result is a weak-baseline win. Blind human graders where explanation scoring is used. Analyze intention-to-treat and attrition; do not treat time-on-task differences as evidence of superiority.

## Critical confounds

* The protocol gives ThinkAI additional retrieval/transfer practice; that may be the treatment, but then equalize opportunity or state the mechanism honestly.
* Near-transfer difficulty may differ accidentally. Pretest pair difficulty and teacher-review it.
* Learners may share answers/tasks; isolate item pools and schedule.
* “Hint level” is correlated with prior knowledge, so it cannot be read causally.
* A delayed test after 3–7 days measures only that schedule, not durable learning generally.

## Ethics and consent

With minors, use school/guardian process as applicable, minimum data, pseudonymous IDs, no model training by default, deletion/retention dates, voluntary withdrawal, and no public ranking. Do not withhold necessary instructional support merely to produce a no-help observation. Teacher review must be real, not a rubber stamp.
